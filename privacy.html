Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how Griffentis LLC ("Griffentis," "we," "us") handles information in connection with this website. We are committed to protecting the privacy of visitors and clients.

We use the information described above to respond to your inquiries, provide and improve our services, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

We retain correspondence and related information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law.
