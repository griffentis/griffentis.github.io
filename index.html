Legend says the Ritae Griffenticas is ancestor to the tiny guardians once kept beside kings: small in stature, sharp in judgment, and impossible to distract from what mattered.

Perched above the valley, she watched over winding paths, hidden risks, and decisions that could shape entire kingdoms. With a narrowed eye and silver crest, she saw the pattern others missed and guided travelers toward the clearest path.

About

Built on deep life sciences experience
